# Warranty & RMA Policy

## Warranty coverage
- All Better2gether watches carry a **2-year limited warranty** from enrollment date
  (`device_registry.enrollment_date`).
- Covered: manufacturing defects, sensor failure, battery health below 60% of rated capacity
  within the warranty window.
- Not covered: physical/water damage beyond rated IP68, cosmetic wear, lost devices.

## Plan-tier service levels
- **Basic** — mail-in RMA, 7–10 business day turnaround.
- **Plus** — expedited RMA, advance replacement after hold authorization.
- **Premium** — next-business-day advance replacement, no hold.

## When to open a battery-health RMA
Open an RMA when rapid battery drain persists **after** the device is confirmed on firmware
**v2.4.1** (i.e., the TS-04 firmware fix did not resolve it). Do not RMA a v2.3.8 device before
attempting the OTA update — most drain complaints resolve with the update.

## RMA process
1. Confirm warranty status by enrollment date and model.
2. Confirm firmware is current (rules out TS-04 firmware cause).
3. Generate an RMA number in the support console.
4. Set member expectations per their plan-tier service level above.
