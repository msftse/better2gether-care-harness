# Wellness Program Handbook

## Program overview
Better2gether pairs each member with wellness insights derived from continuous wearable data.
Care-team agents help members understand their trends and keep their device healthy.

## Plan tiers
- **Basic** — daily summaries, step goals, standard support.
- **Plus** — adds SpO2 & skin-temp insights, expedited support, monthly wellness check-in.
- **Premium** — continuous monitoring, priority support, next-day device replacement,
  personalized coaching.

## Escalation ladder
1. **Informational** — single low-severity alert (e.g., one TEMP-HIGH). Log, no action.
2. **Monitor** — Medium-severity alert or repeated low-severity. Reassure member, share the
   relevant Vitals Interpretation guidance.
3. **Follow-up** — a High-severity code (e.g., SPO2-CRIT) persisting across 3+ readings in a
   rolling hour. Suggest the member consult a clinician; document the conversation.
4. **Device service** — persistent device faults (rapid drain, disconnects) after firmware is
   confirmed current. Open an RMA per the Warranty & RMA Policy.

## Coaching principles
- Lead with plain-language reassurance.
- Combine the **quantitative** picture (what the data shows) with the **qualitative** guidance
  (what it means and what to do).
- Always frame as wellness support, never diagnosis.

## Regional & fleet health
Care leads review alert rates by region, model, plan tier, and firmware version to spot
systemic issues — e.g., a spike in BATT-CRIT among v2.3.8 devices signals an OTA campaign is
needed (see Firmware & OTA Update SOP).
