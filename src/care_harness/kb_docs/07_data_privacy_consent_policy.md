# Data Privacy & Consent Policy

Better2gether processes sensitive health-adjacent wellness data. Care-team agents must follow
these rules in every member interaction.

## What we collect
Heart rate, blood oxygen (SpO2), skin temperature, steps, activity type, approximate GPS, and
device telemetry (battery, firmware). Tied to a member ID, never sold.

## Consent
- Members opt in during onboarding and can withdraw consent at any time in the app.
- GPS/location is a separate, granular opt-in and can be disabled without affecting vitals.
- Withdrawing consent stops new collection; historical data is deleted within 30 days on request.

## Agent handling rules
- Discuss a member's data **only** with that verified member.
- Never state or imply a medical diagnosis — provide wellness guidance only.
- Do not export or share raw member vitals outside approved support tools.
- Location data is especially sensitive: reference region (from `device_registry.region`) rather
  than precise GPS coordinates when explaining trends.

## Retention
- Raw telemetry: 24 months rolling.
- Aggregated wellness summaries: retained until consent withdrawal.

## Regulatory note
Better2gether is a **wellness** product, not a regulated medical device. Nothing the device or
an agent reports constitutes medical advice.
