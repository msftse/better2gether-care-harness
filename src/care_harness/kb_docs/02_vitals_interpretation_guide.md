# Vitals Interpretation Guide (Wellness Framing)

**Important:** Better2gether devices provide wellness insights, not medical diagnoses. All
guidance below is for supporting member conversations. Members with health concerns should
consult a licensed clinician.

## §1. Heart Rate (HR)
- Typical resting heart rate for adults: 60–100 bpm.
- Values above 150 bpm while resting or sleeping raise the **HR-HIGH** alert. Common benign
  causes: caffeine, stress, recent exercise still "cooling down," dehydration.
- Sustained elevated resting HR over multiple days is worth a clinician conversation.
- During `running` and `cycling` activities, high HR is expected and does **not** raise an alert.

## §2. Blood Oxygen (SpO2)
- Normal SpO2 for most people is 95–100%.
- Readings of 94.3–94.9% raise **SPO2-LOW** (monitor). Frequently caused by cold hands, a
  loose band, motion, or measuring during sleep position changes.
- Readings below 94.3% raise **SPO2-CRIT**. Confirm fit and re-measure while still. If the
  member reports shortness of breath, chest tightness, or dizziness, advise contacting a
  clinician promptly.
- SpO2 accuracy degrades when battery is critically low (see Alert Glossary, BATT-CRIT).

## §3. Skin Temperature
- Skin temperature is **not** core body temperature. It drifts with room temperature,
  blankets, and activity.
- Values ≥ 37.3°C raise **TEMP-HIGH** (low severity). Interpret alongside HR and the member's
  reported symptoms. A single TEMP-HIGH reading is rarely actionable on its own.

## §4. Steps & Activity
- The device classifies activity as resting, sleeping, walking, running, or cycling.
- Step counts and activity context are used to suppress false alerts (e.g., high HR during a run).

## Talking to members
Lead with reassurance, describe what the reading means in plain language, and only suggest
clinician follow-up when severity and persistence warrant it. Never state or imply a diagnosis.
